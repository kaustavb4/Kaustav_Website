# Mechanism Prototype

This project involved the complete design lifecycle of a 3-axis gimbal mechanism intended for drone camera stabilization.

## process

1.  **CAD Design**: Modeled in SolidWorks with attention to tolerance and fit.
2.  **Prototyping**: 3D printed initial iterations to verify range of motion and clearance.
3.  **Fabrication**: Machined final aluminum parts for rigidity and heat dissipation.
4.  **Integration**: Integrated brushless DC motors and controller board.

The final prototype successfully stabilized a 500g payload during flight tests.
