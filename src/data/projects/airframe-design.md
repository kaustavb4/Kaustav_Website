# Airframe Design Study

In this project, I focused on the aerodynamic analysis and structural optimization of a UAV airframe. The goal was to maximize lift-to-drag ratio while maintaining structural integrity under high-g maneuvers.

## Key Features

- **CFD Analysis**: Utilized ANSYS Fluent to simulate airflow and optimize wing geometry.
- **Structural Optimization**: Conducted FEA to reduce weight by 15% whilst maintaining safety factors.
- **Material Selection**: Evaluated carbon fiber composites vs. aluminum alloys for strength-to-weight tradeoffs.

This project demonstrated the importance of iterative design and simulation in aerospace engineering.
